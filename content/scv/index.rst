Super Cassette Vision
=====================

:lang: en
:slug: index

The Epoch_ Super Cassette Vision was a home video game console released in Japan on July 17, 1984 and later in France. In total, `30 games`_ were released for the Super Cassette Vision, after which Epoch exited the video game console market.
