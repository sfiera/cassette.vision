Cassette Vision
===============

:lang: en
:slug: index

The Epoch_ Cassette Vision was a home video game console released in Japan on July 30, 1981. In total, `11 games`_ were released for the Cassette Vision, after which Epoch released the `Super Cassette Vision`_.
