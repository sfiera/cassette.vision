Description
-----------

Astro Command is a side-scrolling shooting game modeled after Konami_’s 1981 arcade game Scramble_. The player’s ship has limited energy, and must destroy energy tanks to replenish it. A single hit will destroy the player’s ship.

.. _Konami: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konami 
.. _Scramble: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scramble_(video_game)

Controls
~~~~~~~~

The game controls are:

* Lever (either): move ship up (left) or down (right)
* Button 1 or 3: fire missile
* Button 2 or 4: move ship right. Without this, the ship will remain stationary with respect to the background.
* Start: begin game (outside game) or reset game (during game)
* Select: switch between mode 1 (beginner, 6 lives) or mode 2 (advanced, 4 lives)

Zones
~~~~~

The game proceeds in a loop through 4 zones:

1. The space city, with skyscrapers, interceptor missiles, and mines
2. The labyrinth, with tight maneuvering between walls
3. The enemy base, with tomahawk depth charges and photon torpedo blasters
4. The mothership, with an electromagnetic barrier and an enemy fusion reactor

Points
~~~~~~

Points are awarded as follows:

* Missiles and depth charges:

  * 10 points when stationary
  * 30 points when moving
  * 20 points when the player’s ship is hit

* Energy tanks: 9 points
* Mothership: 100 points per unit of energy remaining

The score display shows 4 digits. If the player scores more than 9999 points, the score changes color, to blue (10000–19999), teal (20000–29999), or purple (30000–39999), after which it loops back to white.

Trivia
------
