Light Gun
=========

:lang: en
:slug: hard/gun

The Light Gun peripheral is modeled after a `Mauser C96 pistol`_. It is shared with Epoch’s earlier System 10 console. The only Cassette Vision game that works with the light gun is `Big Sports 12`_. On the original `Cassette Vision`_, it plugs into the “Connector” port above the “Course” slider. The `Cassette Vision Jr.`_ lacks a connector port, and cannot use the light gun.
