ROM Cartridge
=============

:lang: en
:slug: mod/romcart

.. image:: {static}romcart.png
   :alt: ROM Cartridge PCB
   :width: 300
   :align: center

This is a basic cartridge accepting AT28C64, AT28C256, or compatible EEPROMs, with optional socketing. It supports five configurations, selectable with solder jumpers:

* 8 kiB (compatible with `Astro Bomber`_, `Block Maze`_, Reversi_, and Sokoban_)
* 16 kiB (compatible with Mahjongg_)
* 32 kiB
* 4×8 kiB multi-cart
* 2×16 kiB multi-cart
