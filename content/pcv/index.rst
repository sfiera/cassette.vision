Game Pocket Computer
====================

:lang: en
:slug: index

The Epoch_ Game Pocket Computer (Game Pokekon) was a handheld video game console released in Japan in September, 1984. In total, `5 games`_ were released for the Pokekon, 4 of which were launch titles.

.. _Epoch: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epoch_Co.
