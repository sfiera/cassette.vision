Specifications
--------------

Controls
````````

* Circular directional pad
* 6 buttons (start, select, 4 actions)
* Power switch
* Sound switch
* Contrast dial

Power
`````

* Batteries: 4×AA
* Power draw: 150mW
* Time: ~30 hours
* AC adapter:

  * Voltage: 6VDC
  * Amerage: ≥25mA
  * Polarity: center-minus

The specs for the AC adapter are the same as the `Cassette Vision`_ (original, not Super).

.. _Cassette Vision: https://cassette.vision

Size
````

* Dimensions: 215×146×28.5mm
* Weight: 470g
